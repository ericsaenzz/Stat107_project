05_Cleaning_data.Rmd outlines the data cleaning and preprocessing for our investment performance analysis.
The datasets include historical Bitcoin prices, S&P 500 values, U.S. Treasury ETF prices, and 10-Year treasury yields.
The objective is to prepare consistent datasets for analysis and portfolio comparisons.


Before cleaning the datasets, we examine their dimensions, variable names, and missing observations.
Because the dataset contains no missing values and is a clean dataset from a reputable source our next step is to filter for our analysis.
Our research focuses on the period beginning January 1, 2020, and ending October 1, 2026.
